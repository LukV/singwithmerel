# 003 Layout

**Decision.** The lesson scrolls on the left as a path of numbered exercises; the instrument (staff and keys) stays fixed on the right. On a phone in portrait the lesson is on top and the instrument docks at the bottom. The first screen is the identity. Luk, 2026-11-13; phone layout proposed by Claude, 2026-11-13.

**Why.** The lesson is a workbook, the instrument is a tool; separating them keeps both legible. Score above, keys under the hands is how a pianist sits.
