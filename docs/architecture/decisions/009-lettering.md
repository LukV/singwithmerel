# 009 Lettering

**Decision.** Merel launches with Kalam Bold for hand-lettered headlines, in capitals. A custom alphabet drawn with a marker may replace it later; it is not planned. The wordmark stays a drawing. Luk, 2026-11-14.

**Why.** Kalam is free, has the French accents, and is close enough to the reference to carry release 1. A custom font costs time that is better spent on the app.
