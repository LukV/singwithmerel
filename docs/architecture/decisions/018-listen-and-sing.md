# 018 Listen and Sing

**Decision.** A singing card has two buttons and no steps: Listen and Sing. Listen is Merel singing the melody by name, as often as you like; it never counts. Sing is a switch: on, it is your turn and Merel listens; the last note found earns the dot, and the switch turns off. Without a microphone (said no, refused, missing or failing), you sing on your own and turn Sing off when you are done, and that earns the dot. Start, Again, Next round, Stop and the Sing along step are gone. Completed cards stay open: you can always listen or sing there; the next level still opens only through five dots or Skip this level. Recognition (exercise 4) keeps its questions, which Listen starts, and has no Sing. Luk, 2026-11-16. This replaces the round of Hear it, Sing along and Your turn ([[007-rounds]] keeps the dots and melodies), and the dot after Sing along without a microphone ([[014-your-turn-earns-the-dot]]).

**Why.** Three steps that run by themselves felt like being examined: Merel sang, then sang again, then you had to sing at once. With two buttons you hear the melody as often as you need, and have a go when you are ready, which is how people practise. Sing along added little once Listen can be pressed again at any time; anyone can still hum along.

**What it costs.** Without a microphone, the dot means "I sang it" on your word, not after hearing Merel through, which is the same trust as before. Exercise 3 draws its next melody after each dot, so you listen to the new one before you sing.
