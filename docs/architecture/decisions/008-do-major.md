# 008 Do major, any octave

**Decision.** All exercises stay in do major for now, and the judge accepts a note in any octave. Claude proposed, Luk agreed, 2026-11-14.

**Why.** In do major fixed and movable do give the same names, so one app serves English, French and Dutch teaching. Folding octaves means a bass and a child are judged the same way. Since [[016-movable-do]], do is also wherever you start.
