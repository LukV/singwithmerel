# 014 Your turn earns the dot

**Decision.** From 0.2.0, when Merel can listen, a round's dot comes from Your turn: every note of the melody sung in order, each held within the judge's tolerance. While you sing, a soft dot on the staff follows your voice and each note fills in its colour once you hold it. A miss keeps the notes you found, says something mild, and starts Your turn again from the first note not yet found; tries are unlimited, and after two Merel offers Hear it. Without a microphone the dot comes from Sing along, as in 0.1.0. Both listening stages of [[005-evaluation]] ship together in 0.2.0. Luk, 2026-11-14.

**Why.** A dot should mean you sang it, or the microphone decides nothing. Keeping what you found and trying again without limit follows the rule never to frustrate: nothing resets, nothing is lost, and Skip this level is always there. Seeing your voice next to the note is how people learn to tune; it is a guide, not a score, so it has no needle and no number. A steady tone alone tells a learner little, so it is not worth a release of its own.

**Rejected.** The dot after Sing along with the judge as a bonus (gentlest, but listening would change nothing). A dot after a few tries regardless (no one gets stuck, but the dot no longer means you sang it). Notes filling without a voice dot (calmer, but you cannot see whether you are high or low).
