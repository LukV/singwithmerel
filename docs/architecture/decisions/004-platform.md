# 004 Platform

**Decision.** A static, installable web app at singwithmerel.com (registered in Route 53; hosting in [[010-hosting]]); no accounts, no server, progress in local storage; English, French and Dutch. Luk, 2026-11-14.

**Why.** Cheap to run, nothing to maintain, no personal data. merel.be and merel.com were taken; singwithmerel.com says what the app does in all three languages.
