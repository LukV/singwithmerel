# 007 Rounds, dots and melodies

**Decision.** A melody has three notes in one of three shapes: up-up, down-down, or up then below the start note. One dot is one completed round; five dots complete a level and open the next. Luk, 2026-11-14.

**Why.** This is how melodic dictation is taught in class: first hear higher or lower, then name the note. A dot per round keeps progress visible without counting single notes.
