# Vision

**In brief.** Merel makes singing and hearing notes something anyone can practise for five minutes on a phone, without a teacher, a piano or an account. The concept fits in two lines: "You play. He sings." Then he sings and you find the note. Merel never frustrates: he judges mildly, failing costs nothing, and the app keeps working without a microphone.

## Who it is for

All ages, no musical background needed. A child who starts music school, an adult who always wanted to sing in tune, a teacher who wants a homework tool. Luk is the first user: he wants to learn it himself.

## Five rules

These decide every trade-off in the product and the code.

1. **Never frustrate.** Judging is mild, a wrong answer never resets a round, a missing microphone never blocks progress, and nothing shows a score.
2. **Nothing leaves the device.** No audio is stored or sent, there is no tracking, and the app says so on screen.
3. **No accounts.** Progress lives in the browser.
4. **Cheap to run.** Static files on a free host; the domain is the only cost.
5. **Music first.** Colour supports real notation and never replaces it: a note has a clef, a stem and a place in a bar, and its colour marks only that you played or sang it.

## What Merel is not

Not a game with points, streaks or leaderboards. Not a piano course: the keys are there to find and check notes, not to learn to play. Not a social app: nothing is shared.
